// about the department

A Living Data Intelligence

Statistics and Data Science is not just a department — it is a living ecosystem of data intelligence. Founded on the belief that statistics is the language of reality, we train the next generation of data scientists, statisticians, and AI researchers.

Our curriculum bridges classical statistical theory with cutting-edge machine learning, deep neural architectures, and real-world data engineering. We exist at the intersection of mathematics, computation, and human insight.
